## Scripts for making figures in "Impact of future aircraft NOx emissions on atmospheric composition and climate: dependence on background conditions", Staniaszek et al. 2026

Fig 1: NOx_emissions_plots.ipynb, NOx emissions present day and future (SSP1 and SSP3 in 2050)

Fig 2: rfs_present_future_burden.ipynb, rfs_present_future_new.ipynb, Present day, SSP1 and SSP3 ozone RF bar charts, absolute and scaled by NOx emissions, change in burdens.

Fig 3: o3_conc_rf_future.ipynb, calculation of ozone RFs from ozone mixing ratios. Plots of ozone RF including vertical distribution and global means.

Fig 4: o3_conc_rf_future.ipynb, calculation of ozone RFs from ozone mixing ratios. Plots of ozone RF including zonal mean distribution and vertical distribution.

Fig 5: net_nox_future.ipynb, Net NOx ERF bar charts with hatching for SSP1 background.
